## Generation constraints

Each primary image was used as a strict product-identity reference. Prompts requested a different three-quarter detail angle in warm natural light while preserving the demonstrated shape, color, material, proportions, and function. They explicitly prohibited added functions, text, logos, watermarks, hands, and people.

- **The Little Horizon:** same pale mustard-yellow matte arch stand and phone slot; side angle showing the arch profile; no charging or adjustable parts.
- **The Pebble Pause:** same olive-green stone-like asymmetric pebble form and single pen groove; close side detail; no added compartments.
- **The Cloud Rest:** same warm off-white three-lobed cloud form and single curved cable groove; side detail; no ports, indicators, or charging implication.
