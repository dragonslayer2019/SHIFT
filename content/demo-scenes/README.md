# SHIFT demo scene assets

These fictional demo scenes were generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-28/29 for the SHIFT development storefront. They are not photographs of customers, customer homes, or current products and must be reviewed or replaced before a production launch.

Each scene is retained as an original PNG in `source/` and as an optimized JPEG in `web/`. The JPEGs were uploaded to Shopify Files and selected by the unpublished review theme.

| File | Storefront use | Intended crop |

| `shift-home-hero` | Homepage hero | Portrait 4:5; sofa and desk held near the center for narrow crops |
| `shift-living-spaces` | Living spaces entry card and collection cover | Landscape 4:3 |
| `shift-desk-spaces` | Desk spaces entry card and collection cover | Landscape 4:3 |

## Prompt record

All prompts requested photorealistic-natural editorial scenes for American small-space rentals, using warm cream, muted coral, leafy green, pale oak, and restrained forest-green accents. They prohibited logos, text, watermarks, luxury architecture, and exact copies of current store products.

- **Homepage hero:** compact rental studio with distinct living and desk zones, no people, central composition that preserves both zones in a narrow mobile crop.
- **Living spaces:** compact studio lounge with an oatmeal sofa, movable nesting tables, slim open shelf, and clear circulation path.
- **Desk spaces:** multipurpose rental desk with an open work surface, closed laptop, notebook, headphones, lamp, books, and plant; no gaming/RGB setup.
- **Living use case:** a lounge area visually anchored by a rug and freestanding shelf while leaving the route to the sleeping area open.
- **Desk use case:** an early-evening desk reset with a closed laptop and clear landing spots, leaving the center available for another activity.

No generated scene should be interpreted as a product specification, installation example, testimonial, or depiction of a current SHIFT item.
